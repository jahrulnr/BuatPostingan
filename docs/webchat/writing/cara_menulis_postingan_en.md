# How to write a post

Short guide for drafting posts in BuatPostingan.

## Clear titles

- Prefer specific titles over generic ones.
- Aim for 6–12 words; put the main topic first.

## Body structure

1. Open with one short paragraph that answers why the topic matters.
2. Break the body into `##` subheadings, one idea each.
3. Close with a concrete call to action (not “hope this helps”).

## Pre-publish checklist

- Spelling and product names checked.
- Images have alt text.
- Internal and external links still work.
- Final CTA matches the post goal.
